### 🧠 Overview

This repository contains my **Analogue Electronics** laboratory work, documenting the design, simulation, and analysis of key analog circuits using **operational amplifiers**, **instrumentation amplifiers**, **integrators**, **differentiators**, and **transistor amplifiers**.

Each lab includes:
- Circuit schematics and LTSpice simulations  
- Theoretical analysis and calculations  
- Measured results and discussion  
- A full technical report in PDF format  

---

### 🔬 Labs Completed So Far

#### 🧩 **Lab 1 – Operational Amplifiers**
- Investigated **inverting** and **non-inverting** amplifier configurations  
- Verified theoretical gain and phase response experimentally  
- Compared measured output to simulation results in **LTSpice**

#### 🎛️ **Lab 2 – Instrumentation Amplifier**
- Built a **3-op-amp instrumentation amplifier** for small differential signal measurement  
- Verified common-mode rejection and gain accuracy  

#### ⚙️ **Lab 3 – Integrators & Differentiators** *(upcoming)*
- Will explore op-amp-based **RC feedback networks** performing integration and differentiation  
- Analyze frequency-domain behaviour and waveform shaping

#### 💡 **Lab 4 – Transistor Amplifiers** *(upcoming)*
- Design and bias a **common-emitter amplifier**  
- Measure voltage and current gain  
- Compare AC small-signal model with real circuit response
